Marketing & Representative Partners

Last updated: June 2026

When you submit a request through The Disability Office, you may — with your prior express consent — be contacted by The Disability Office and by one or more of the disability representatives, advocate organizations, law firms, call centers, and marketing partners described below (our “partners”). They may contact you by telephone, text message, and email at the number and address you provide — including through automated dialing technology and prerecorded or artificial/AI-generated voice — about Social Security Disability benefits and related services, even if your number is on a state or federal Do-Not-Call list. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or of receiving services, and you may revoke it at any time. See our Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and Do Not Sell or Share page.

The Disability Office is a private, independent referral service. We are not the Social Security Administration (SSA) or any government agency, are not affiliated with or endorsed by SSA, and are not a law firm. The representatives we connect you with are independent third parties; whether they are attorneys or non-attorney advocates, contacting us does not create an attorney-client or advocate-client relationship. Any organization names that may appear are the property of their respective owners and are used for identification only. This list may be updated from time to time.
